The corner shows the hand as the machine sees it. Green means it is tracked and being read; amber means a landmark was dropped by a quality gate. A letter is committed only after the shape has been held still for the patience setting — the bar above fills as you hold, and the letter lands when it is full. Moving between letters resets it, which is what stops shapes you pass through from being read as letters you never signed.
